Terra Nota Support

Questions, problems or ideas

Email colin@burn-murdoch.com and I'll get back to you, usually within a couple of days. If something looks wrong on a map, a screenshot helps a lot.

Common questions

My maps aren't on my other device

Maps sync through your own iCloud. Check that both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account and that Terra Nota is allowed to use iCloud in your device’s iCloud settings. Large maps can take a few minutes to arrive.

My position looks off on a map

Add more calibration points, spread out across the map rather than bunched together. The Points list shows how well each one agrees with the rest, so you can fix or remove one that's out. For hand-drawn or stretched maps, try the Flexible fit.

The app can't find my location

Allow location in Settings › Terra Nota › Location. Terra Nota only uses it while the app is open.

Moving to a new phone, or keeping a copy

iCloud moves your maps across on its own. To keep a copy as well, use ⋯ › Export Everything on the Maps list, which saves everything as one file. Import Backup… in the same menu brings it back.

A library map I expected isn't listed

Terra Nota only shows library maps whose licence allows re-use. Some collections don't publish one, so their maps are left out.
